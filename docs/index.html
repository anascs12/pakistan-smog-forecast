About the data, and its limits

PM2.5 values are estimates from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) global model, served by Open-Meteo, not ground sensors. CAMS captures day-to-day swings and the winter smog season, but it is known to underestimate the most extreme readings that ground monitors record in Lahore and Peshawar, so real conditions can be worse than shown.

The model is a gradient-boosting regressor using recent pollution levels and the weather forecast for each day, especially night-time mixing height (low mixing traps smoke near the ground), wind, humidity and temperature. This is a student data-science project, not an official advisory: follow guidance from the Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Environmental Protection Agencies.
